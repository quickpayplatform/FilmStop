Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Film Stop.

What types of projects does Film Stop specialize in?

Film Stop specializes in story-driven video production for artists, brands, and organizations. Our core work includes music videos, commercials, brand content, documentaries, and event coverage. We focus on projects where story, authenticity, and intention matter.

Where are you located, and do you travel?

We're based in Baltimore, Maryland, and regularly work throughout DC, Maryland, and Virginia. We also travel for the right projects. If your story takes us elsewhere, we're open to the conversation.

What does a typical project cost?

Every project is different, but most Film Stop projects fall within these ranges:

  • Music Videos: $2,000–$6,000+
  • Commercials & Brand Content: $2,500–$10,000+
  • Documentaries: $3,500+
  • Event Coverage: starting at $1,500

Final pricing depends on scope, crew size, locations, and post-production needs. We'll build a package that fits your goals and budget.

Do you offer packages or custom pricing?

Yes. All projects are custom-built based on your needs. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all pricing. After an initial consultation, we'll recommend an approach that makes sense creatively and financially.

What's included in your production services?

Our services typically include creative development, pre-production planning, production day coverage, and post-production (editing, color grading, and sound design). Specific inclusions vary by project and are clearly outlined before production begins.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on project scope, but most projects follow this general range:

  • Music Videos & Brand Content: 2–4 weeks
  • Commercials: 3–6 weeks
  • Documentaries: timeline varies based on depth and length

Rush options may be available when needed.

Do you help with creative direction or concepts?

Absolutely. Many clients come to us with an idea, not a fully formed concept. We help shape the story, develop visuals, and guide creative decisions so the final product feels intentional and cohesive.

How does the process start?

It starts with a free creative consultation. We'll talk through your goals, audience, and vision. From there, we outline scope, timeline, and next steps—no pressure, no obligation.

How many revisions are included?

Each project includes a set number of revision rounds, agreed upon upfront. Our goal is to get it right early through clear planning, so revisions are focused and efficient.

Do you work with first-time artists or small brands?

Yes. We work with both emerging creatives and established organizations. What matters most is that you care about your story and are serious about making something meaningful.

Do you offer ongoing or retainer-based work?

For brands and artists releasing content consistently, we offer ongoing content packages and retainers. These are ideal for social content, campaigns, and long-term storytelling.

How do we book Film Stop?

You can book a consultation directly through the website or reach out via email or phone. Once we align on scope and vision, we'll secure your date and move into pre-production.

What makes Film Stop different from other production studios?

We don't chase trends or recycle concepts. Film Stop is built on intentional storytelling, clear communication, and respect for the craft. We care about what the video does—not just how it looks.
