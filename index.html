Our Story

Why Film Stop Exists

Film Stop started because we saw too many artists and brands settle for generic content. Cookie-cutter videos that look like everything else. We knew there was a better way — productions that actually tell your story, not just fill a content calendar.

After 9 years and 649+ projects, our philosophy hasn't changed: every project deserves real creative attention. We work best with independent artists, culture-driven brands, and organizations who value authenticity over trends. No bloated agency overhead. No recycled concepts. Just focused, cinematic storytelling.

Based in Baltimore, we serve artists and brands across the DMV and beyond. If you want results without sacrificing craft, start here.

Meet the Founder

Tee Forest

Film Stop started with a phone, a hunger for storytelling, and a first camera funded by a community grant. What began as one person chasing a creative outlet became something bigger when the world shut down in 2020.

During the pandemic, while stages went dark and gatherings stopped, Tee saw an opportunity: video could keep people connected. Film Stop leaned in — sometimes producing three projects a day — helping clients maintain their presence when physical presence wasn't an option. One standout: partnering with Step Afrika, DC's renowned stepping company, to bring their electrifying performances to a global digital audience when live shows weren't possible. We became the bridge between their artistry and their community, without losing what made them authentic.

That season shaped everything. Now, as the world has reopened, the belief remains: stories are how we remember. They're what connect us to yesterday and point us toward tomorrow. That's what Film Stop is built on — not just making videos, but making moments that matter last.
