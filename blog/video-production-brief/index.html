How to write a video production brief

Give your producer a clear audience, one job for the video, and the constraints that shape the shoot.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Give the video one job

Write one sentence that names the viewer and what you want them to do. For example, “Help prospective donors understand our after-school program and visit the donation page.” That gives the director a basis for choosing scenes and gives your team a way to evaluate the edit.

Name the evidence you can show

List the people, places, and actions that make the story credible. For the after-school program, that could be a mentor showing how students work through a project, a student describing a specific breakthrough, and classroom footage that shows the work happening. Flag access or permissions early.

Specify where the film will live

A silent lobby loop, a homepage feature, and an Instagram Reel have different requirements. Include placements, target lengths, aspect ratios, caption needs, and any mandatory brand elements. Mark the assets you need at launch.

Define the approval path

Name the person who can approve the brief and consolidate edit notes. Include the actual launch date, your budget range, and any date when the people or spaces you need are available. A producer can schedule around a deadline once those dependencies are visible.
