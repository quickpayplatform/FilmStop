What does a video production company do?

See what a production team handles before the shoot, on set, and after filming wraps.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Discuss your production

Before filming

The producer turns the brief into a plan people can execute. That may include developing the idea, writing questions or a script, scouting locations, and figuring out how to get the right people into the room. You should know what you’ll be asked to approve before the crew arrives.

On set

The team manages camera, lighting, and sound while the director works with the people on screen. In a normal office, that can mean pausing for elevator noise or moving a setup so staff can keep working. A good schedule gives the crew room to handle those adjustments.

After the shoot

The editor finds the story in the footage, then builds a cut for review. Once the team approves the structure, the crew can finish color, sound, captions, and graphics. If there are different formats for web, social, or an event screen, each needs a final check.

Where the client comes in

Name a contact who can confirm access and one person who can consolidate feedback. Share brand files, names and titles, and any requirements for final delivery early. You can handle parts of the work in-house if everyone agrees on the handoff.
