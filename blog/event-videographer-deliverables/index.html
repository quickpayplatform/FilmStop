Event video recap or full recording? What to book

How to choose the right event video coverage for attendees, sponsors, and the next event.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Plan your event video package

Book a recap when you need the feeling of the room

A recap combines a few strong moments with reactions, location details, and short pieces of speech or interview. Give the crew one reason the event matters. That helps them find moments with a story instead of shooting every table and sign.

Book full recordings when the talks are the asset

If people will watch a session later, plan for steady coverage and clean audio throughout. Ask whether slides should appear in the final file and how audience questions will be recorded. Confirm which sessions need coverage if they run at the same time.

Give sponsor clips their own plan

Decide what each partner should receive and when. If a sponsor needs a short interview or a look at an activation, give the crew time and a point of contact. Check name spelling and sponsor marks before the event so the edit can move quickly.

Set two deadlines if you need speed and depth

You might need a short recap the next morning and longer edits later. Choose the handful of shots and quotes that must make the quick cut. Give the longer work its own review schedule.
