Corporate video cost

How to budget for a one-day corporate video shoot

Build a budget around what can realistically fit in a day and what the editor needs afterward.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Get a starting estimate

Count setups before you count interviews

Three interviews in the same quiet room can be straightforward. The same three people across two buildings add travel, resets, and fresh sound checks. List every room or look you need and how long each person is available.

Put must-have footage on the schedule first

For a customer story, protect the main interview and one scene that shows what the customer is describing. Add supporting footage if the timing works. If you need a product demo, block time to reset it and capture details from a second angle.

Budget the work after everyone goes home

Include the rough cut, reviews, audio, color, captions, and delivery. Decide whether the same footage will support social edits. Give each extra cut a clear purpose and confirm the producer included it in the estimate.

Ask for a base scope and options

A base scope could cover a single location, interviews, and one final edit. Ask for separate options for another setup or an extra cut. You’ll be able to see where the money goes and decide which additions are worth paying for.
