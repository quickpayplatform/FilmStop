How to plan an independent music video

Turn a track into a shootable treatment and plan the main video, teaser, and vertical clips before release week.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Build the treatment around the song

Pick the moment where the song changes emotionally. It might be a beat drop, a bridge, or the first line of a chorus. Decide what the audience should see at that moment. A visual shift in performance, light, or location can make that transition memorable.

Choose performance setups you can finish

Plan the setup that will carry the full track, then add one or two distinct looks where they earn screen time. Bring the final playback file and make sure the performer has rehearsed to that version. Confirm wardrobe and any props before call time.

Plan the release clips during pre-production

Choose the lines or moments for a teaser, release-day clip, and follow-up. If a wide setup puts the artist at the edge of frame, shoot a vertical pass while the lights and performer are ready. Capture a still with enough space for release information if the artwork calls for it.

Work backward from the release

Leave room for feedback, color, exports, and upload. Have the artist or a designated team member collect notes in one pass, and make sure the delivery list includes any clean or captioned versions needed for distribution.

Bring Film Stop your track

Send a private track link, your release date, and a couple of references. Tell us which part of the song you most want people to remember.
