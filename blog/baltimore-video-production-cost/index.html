How much does a corporate video cost?

What changes a corporate video quote, with examples of how to scope a project before you ask for pricing.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Open the quote calculator

Start with the project you can describe

A one-location customer interview with a short edit has a different scope from a campaign with three sites and several versions. Write down what you want to film and what you need at delivery. If you know when the final files are due, include that too.

For a starting point, Film Stop lists commercial production at $2,500 to $10,000+. That is our published range for commercials, and a corporate project can land at a different number depending on the brief. The calculator lets you change the scope before you ask for a tailored quote.

Account for preparation and production

A straightforward interview may need a short planning call and location check. A scripted shoot can require a treatment, casting, or more time to set up. Each company quotes differently, so ask who handles those items and whether the proposal includes them.

Look at the edit as its own cost

Captioning, custom graphics, music, and extra versions can all take time after the shoot. Ask whether shorter edits are separate cuts or trims of the master. If several people must approve the project, agree on revision rounds and who will send the notes.

Ask what you can simplify

Keep the people and scenes that tell the story. If you need to cut scope, see whether you can film at one location or deliver the main edit first. Use an estimate to compare those choices, then ask the producer what fits your brief.
