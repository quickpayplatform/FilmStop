Budgeting

What drives the cost of video production in Baltimore?

See which decisions affect a video quote, what to compare across proposals, and where to simplify the scope.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Start with the deliverables

A homepage film, two paid social ads, and a vertical teaser can share footage, but each needs its own edit decisions. List the assets you need, where each will run, and whether captions, alternate versions, or stills are part of delivery. The producer can then plan coverage around the whole package.

Look at what the shoot actually requires

Two interviews in one quiet office can fit a compact setup. Two interviews across town with different room access windows may need more time for travel, lighting, and sound. Add any cast, location fees, rentals, or travel you already know about so the estimate reflects your plan.

Compare the same scope

Check who writes the script, how many revision rounds are included, and whether the quote covers music, graphics, captioning, and delivery. Ask how changes are priced if a second shoot day or new cut becomes necessary.

Protect the shots that tell the story

If the budget is tight, reduce the number of locations or distinct concepts first. An interview with time for good sound and specific supporting footage can carry a stronger film than three rushed setups.
