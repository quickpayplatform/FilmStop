Corporate video production

What type of corporate video does your business need?

Choose between a brand film, customer story, product demo, and recruiting video based on the job it needs to do.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Discuss your video idea

Brand film for an introduction

Use a brand film when people need to understand who you are and how you work. Give the viewer a scene they can remember. For a manufacturing business, a close look at a team solving a problem on the floor can tell you more than a montage of the building.

Customer story for a buying decision

If prospects are asking whether your service works for a business like theirs, find a customer who can talk about a specific before and after. Ask what triggered the decision and what changed day to day. A detail the customer remembers is more persuasive than a string of adjectives.

Product demo for a specific question

Show the action the viewer wants to understand. If there are three steps, film them clearly and in sequence. For a recruiting video, the equivalent is showing what a workday looks like and letting an employee explain what new hires can expect.

Choose the first place it will run

A homepage visitor, a sales prospect opening an email, and a candidate scrolling on a phone all arrive with different context. Pick the first placement before you write. It will help you decide what to show in the opening and what the viewer should do next.
