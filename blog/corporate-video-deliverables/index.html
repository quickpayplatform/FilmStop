Corporate video production

What deliverables should you ask for on a corporate video shoot?

Plan the main edit, shorter versions, captions, and files your team will need after production.

November 12, 2026 · Film Stop

Plan your corporate shoot

Give each edit a purpose

The homepage film can explain the story in full. A short sales clip might answer one common objection. A vertical clip may have one good line and a clear next step. List what each version needs to do so the crew can capture the right opening and ending.

Plan framing while the camera is out

Some wide shots crop to vertical with no trouble. Two people sitting at opposite ends of a frame usually need another setup. Tell the crew early if a key demo or interview will need a vertical version so they can frame for it on set.

Get specific about captions and files

Ask for the formats your team can publish, including separate caption files or versions with captions built in. Check whether you need a clean version for an event, an alternate end card, or an archive master. Ask about raw footage separately if your internal team plans to edit later.

Use a one-page delivery list

Write one row per asset with the platform, length, aspect ratio, captions, and due date. Share it with whoever approves the video and whoever uploads it. That way a missing vertical cut is easier to catch before the crew wraps.

Scope your deliverables

Share your publishing channels and launch dates. We can build a shoot plan that covers the edits you’ll use.
